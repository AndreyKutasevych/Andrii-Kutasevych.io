Summary

An English-language and a Russian-language Discord account-compromise casino scam were investigated to see whether one operator runs both.

Key judgment (high confidence): the two campaigns share backend infrastructure, not just tactics. Both resolve through overlapping hosting blocks (KnownSRV Ltd., Netherlands and Cherry Servers/IPXO, Lithuania), share a live admin panel, and sit on IPs that serve tens of thousands of DNS resolutions for dozens of "mellstroy" domains alongside the English-side pivot domain.

No named threat actor or APT matches this operation in any source checked (Intel471, AIM CTI, underground forums). The operator looks like an independent or small-team infrastructure reseller with an 8+ year history on the same hosting footprint, moving from adult-content pay-per-download affiliate programs to casino scams.

The two campaigns

Both use compromised Discord accounts that DM the victim's contacts with fake casino-winning screenshots, localized per language.

The Mellstroy cluster

Passive DNS on the hosts behind api.drgn43[.]casino and gammonef[.]com showed hundreds of "mellstroy" and "drgn" domains. This is the direct evidence linking the two campaigns: the English-side pivot domain shares a server with dozens of actively resolving Russian-campaign domains.

On 185.66.140.132 (same /22 as drgn43)

  • mellstroy-igame[.]bet: 14,216 records (4-20 Aug 2026), the flagship active domain.
  • Live: wmellstroy[.]com, kickmellstroy[.]com, mellstroygame43[.]com, mellstroygambling[.]com and several .top variants.
  • Burners: drgn20, drgn38, drgn50, drgn61, drgn75, drgn77 across multiple TLDs.
  • Historical: heroturko1[.]com (2015), andrioshacks[.]com (2017), minirlss[.]net (2014).

  • wlfcasino[.]com bridges both IPs (registrar REG.RU LLC). mellbot.botvgrs[.]online looks like a bot subdomain.

Admin panel

adminka.gammonef[.]com was found while enumerating gammonef[.]com subdomains. "Adminka" is Russian slang for admin panel, and the site sets a language=ru cookie on first contact. Findings are from passive HTTP, TLS and DNS recon of a public page only. No credentials were tried.
